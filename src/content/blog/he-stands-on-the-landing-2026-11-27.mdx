On a wet stone landing far inside the keep stands a small rusted robot, on his own two feet. This is Rustboy. His one green eye glows beside an empty socket, and his chest carries a round port. His rounded feet are planted near the edge of the landing. He faces partly toward us. At the left, a stair rises, and light falls down it from above.

This is a revisit. The still and clip below ran first on 29 October, in "He stands anyway," back when the film was in an older cut. Our last three posts covered the sits that come earlier. Today we return to this view in the 5 November film.

## Upright again

For a while, the film keeps him low. In our last three posts, he sits on a metal landing, then pauses on the spiral stair, then sits on wet steps. This shot shows him on his feet again. That one change turns the picture around. Seated, he looks up, looks down, or looks out at us. Standing, he is back on his feet, with the lit stairs rising beside him.

The shot gives him nothing more than that. The pauses before it offer him no task. Standing gives him a new posture, not an answer. He is still a robot without a job, upright on a landing, with light ahead.

The shot sits at about 1:36 to 1:42 in the published film. Just before it, he sits on a landing ledge and looks into a rain puddle. Just after it, he climbs the last steps slowly.

Nothing in this post is new production work. No new pictures came out of today, the still and clip were posted in October, and the 5 November film already carries this shot.
