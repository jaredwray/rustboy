## Story beat card

- **Who:** Rustboy, the leftover scrap kid with one green eye, and the fox he freed.
- **Where:** A wet rock shelf by the draining tide, then a coast path leading away from the keep, the dead fortress where he woke up.
- **What is happening:** He doesn't go back. He climbs out of the water, sits alone and touches the rim of his empty eye socket, then walks on toward the horizon, having helped.
- **Why it matters:** He went looking for a purpose and found it in helping other lives. It didn't make him whole, and he walks on anyway.

The tide has let go of him. Rustboy climbs out of the channel where he helped the fox and the storm-petrel, and sits alone on a flat wet shelf of rock. He touches the edge of his empty eye socket. He doesn't cover it. The fox is free and far away, going her own way.

Then he gets up and walks. The keep, the fortress where he woke with no purpose, sits small behind him, and he doesn't look back. Ahead of him the coast opens out toward the horizon. He helped, he isn't whole, and he keeps going.

## The story order changed

The team settled the order of the closing scenes. The quiet moment alone on the shelf (absence) and the walk away from the keep (keep-behind) now come right after he climbs out of the water. An earlier version of the absence moment (`kf23t`) has been retired from the film. The horizon shot that closes the reel is a new keyframe, `kf25j`. A keyframe, or plate, is one finished picture that locks how a moment looks.

## The new cut

A progress cut is the single reel that puts every finished shot in story order and carries the music and sound mix. This one, MIX_v16, is the first with the new order, about 175 seconds long.
