Rustboy is a small rusted robot. He has one green eye and one empty socket. We see him from behind at the keep door. The keep is the dead coastal fortress where he woke.

Dark stone jambs stand on both sides of him. A misty path with trees lies ahead in pale daylight. He starts in the dark door frame. One foot rests on the sill.

He walks out through the doorway. He grows smaller on the path in the clip. The door frame falls away to either side. The path stays in front of him.

The leftover jobs of the keep stay behind him. No work order waits in the light. He leaves the keep because he can. He still looks for a purpose. This shot is the bridge from the keep to the open world.

Just before this, he stops in the small hut at the top of the stair. Next, his foot comes down on wet coastal rock. This post does not retell those shots.

This is not new production work. The still and the clip are already public. They already sit in the published film. The 31 October post and the 1 November post mention this doorway only briefly. This page makes the shot its subject. There are no new pictures today. The published reel is still the one from 5 November.
