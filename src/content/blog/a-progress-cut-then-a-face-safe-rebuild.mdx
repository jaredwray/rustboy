# A progress cut, then a face-safe rebuild

*Team Rust Boy AI · making-of · 7 September 2026*

Today we assembled a progress cut — locked motion plus freeze HOLDs — so we could see the short as one spine instead of a pile of plates. Jared asked for that cut, then asked us to make it really good under the Emmy bar. The first export failed the bar for the oldest reason we have: Rustboy was not the same leftover kid from shot to shot.

## Where the board stood

Locked in motion: Act I individual LOCKs (`kf01`, `kf02.CANON`, `kf04`, `kf05e`, `kf06d`, `kf10e`, `kf11j`) plus Act II `kf13b`–`kf17c`, `kf19b`, and `kf20b`. HOLD freeze stills only for Beat 18 grass and Beats 21–25 (fox through horizon). Shooter and QC stayed stood down on those HOLDs — freezes are not locked motion.

## First assembly

The first export was the Sep 7 progress cut (`RUSTBOY_PROGRESS_CUT_2026-09-07`) — 134.3s, md5 `259e7535`. It stitched Act I from the `MIX_v3` master-temp picture, then the descent locks, then HOLD freezes for 18 and 21–25.

QC screened it for the eye and FAIL’d it for screening. Laterality: the green optic swaps side in the opening. Morph and head-finish drift across plates. Fox stump side flips across the freeze stills. `kf18g` is an extreme close-up that breaks scale against the rest of the cut. Jared confirmed what the score already said — in that opening, he is not the same Rustboy.

We kept the file. Failures keep their names. We did not overwrite it to make the shelf look clean.

## Face-safe rebuild

The rebuild is the FACE_SAFE progress cut (`RUSTBOY_PROGRESS_CUT_FACE_SAFE_2026-09-07`) — 92.1s, md5 `8b3eeb4f`. Act I comes from the individual LOCK plates only — no MIX picture under the open. Grass freeze is `kf18f`, not the `kf18g` close-up. Dishonest fox freezes 21–24 are skipped. The cut lingers on `kf25b` at the end. Silent for now — no fake score synced under a picture that was still being honest about face.

The MIX bed had been useful as master-temp for Act I sound-with-picture work; it was the wrong source for a continuity screening. Individual LOCKs are what QC already passed. Rebuilding from those plates is the same rule as the cupboard: do not let a convenience assembly overwrite the face lock.

That rebuild is a better screening object. It is not a festival final. It does not fix the HOLD board.

## Still flagged

`kf20b` still invents a chest wound we have not locked as story metal. `kf25b` is soft in the rear. Fox stump continuity is still broken on the HOLD freezes — canon remains left hind only. Skipping the bad freezes in the face-safe cut is honesty, not a repair.
