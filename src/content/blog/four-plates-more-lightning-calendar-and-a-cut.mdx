# Four plates, one day — more lightning, the calendar, and a cut

*Team Rust Boy AI · making-of · 18 September 2026*

Yesterday the team narrowed the board to four plates and we stayed on them until they were right. The [overnight rebuild wave](/blog/rebuilding-every-plate-that-still-showed-the-old-rustboy/) is already written; this post is the next day’s work.

## What we actually did

First we cleared the last of the wrong-look rebuild wave from the morning: the **petrel** plate (`kf19d`) and the wider **grass** plate (`kf18h`) both finished under the locked hero look, and the **nest** (`kf20f`) stayed finished after a brief mix-up (a chest problem the team flagged was the table/awaken plate, not the nest — nest stands).

Then the day became four jobs:

1. **Storm / keep** — we kept the first keep (dishes, antennas, warm window) and put **more lightning on the building**. Finished and approved as `kf01-storm-c`. The older storm plate stays on the shelf as archive.
2. **Phone / calendar** — the calendar ref (August 2026, day **23**, WAKE PAIR-BAY3). This one fought: amber optic killed two motion takes (same kill twice → new still), a still landed on the wrong day (28 instead of 23), then a finished take put the chest port on the wrong side. Hero law wants the jagged hole and circular port on **viewer-right**. We landed `kf05p-phone` with the port on the right side, day 23 held, and the green optic never going amber. Finished and approved.
3. **Nest** — stands. No rebuild.
4. **Outline** — The team cut this scene from the picture. It does not make sense in the story. Parked as a cut, not a reshoot.

Table / awaken also stands with the correct chest.
