Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, has followed a storm-petrel to the wet rock under its nest. He arrives on his feet, beside the bird and the copper flake it took from his chest. Then the film cuts: he is already lying on his back, and the flake is on the rock beside him. Receiving that care comes before he later chooses to help — a fox cut off by rising water, the petrel caught in scrap wire. Helping other lives is where he begins to find a purpose.

## The movement the film does not show

Between those two plates, the published film never shows him lowering himself onto the rock. The jump is from standing to already at rest. We looked at that missing action as research, not as a new plate to shoot. A picture of him already lying down would not be the same thing as watching him choose to lie down. We have not selected or tested a shot of that movement.

Doing so would need a verified native source of the action itself — at least 1280 by 720, without upscaling — that keeps the same Rustboy all the way through. A new background alone could not supply the movement. Until such a source is in hand, the public cut keeps the jump.
