Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, is alone on the top flight of a spiral service stair inside the keep, the dead coastal data fortress where he woke up. He is just above the halfway landing. Storm-light falls from the roof door above. He climbs the last steps slowly, one at a time, and a flake of rust lifts off his chest plate and drifts up into the beam ahead of him.

Why that matters: he chose to stand on the landing, and now he keeps choosing, step by step, worn down but still heading for the light. The flake riding the beam shows how little power he has left, and that he climbs anyway, toward the door at the top where the threshold waits.
