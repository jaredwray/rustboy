## Story beat card

- **Who:** Rustboy, the leftover scrap kid with one green eye, last seen small on the coast.
- **Where:** The open shore at the horizon, then nothing but black.
- **What is happening:** The screen goes black. Then the name of the place he's headed appears: rustboy.ai. The surf keeps going.
- **Why it matters:** He helped, he isn't whole, and he walks on anyway. The film doesn't tell us where he ends up. It cuts away and lets the sea keep sounding, so his walk goes on past the last picture.

Rustboy stands small at the edge of the sea, the keep far behind him. Then the picture is gone. For a moment there is only black, and the waves are still breaking somewhere in the dark. Out of the black comes one small line of rust-orange type: **rustboy.ai**, the name of the place he's headed. The surf doesn't stop when the words appear. It rolls on through the card, as if he's still out there walking.

## The ending is locked

An end card is the final title that closes a film. The team tried a version with the film's name over the web address, and chose the simpler one: just **rustboy.ai**, small, rust-orange on black. That version is now locked, which means it's final and won't be redrawn.

Before the card, the screen holds on black for about a second and a half. That pause gives the horizon shot room to settle before the words come in.

The sound carries the ending too. A surf bar, one musical measure of waves (about four and a half seconds), plays across the black and through the card. The picture now holds on the card until that bar finishes, so no one's video player cuts the sound off early.
