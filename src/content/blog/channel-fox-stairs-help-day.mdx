## The tide channel

The climax sits at the keep’s outflow: scrap and cable dump into a narrow cut between wet stones. The petrel is tangled alive in wire. The fox is free on four legs — no rope — but rising water and a scrap barrier cut her off from high ground. Rustboy stands on a safer higher stone and looks down.

We finished a single photo that locks how that moment looks before any of it becomes a moving clip.
