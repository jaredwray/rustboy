Yesterday the team cleared the sticky tide-channel scene — a finished photo and a short moving clip — and the day kept going until the help climax landed too.

## Tide channel

We spent the morning finishing a photo stand-in for the sticky channel: a finished single photo that locks how the scene looks before any of it becomes a moving clip. A storm-petrel is alive in scrap wire, Rustboy is on a safer higher stone looking down, and a four-leg fox is free beside them (no rope). That photo cleared. A face-to-lens try — him looking straight into the camera — failed twice, so we seeded the short moving clip from a look-down twin of the same frame (the same picture, with his gaze down). The clip cleared. Soft notes only — small leftovers, not blockers: a slightly hot green optic (his one eye running a little bright), a hand near the fox, a shared rock. The sticky channel is **finished** as a picture and a short clip. It is **not** in the screening reel yet.

## Fox lane rebuilt

Under the free four-leg law — the fox unbound, no rope, four legs — the earlier three-leg fox pictures are no longer the guide. We rebuilt four photo stand-ins in story order: first contact across open air, a quiet shared lee (a sheltered spot out of the wind), absence with an empty eye socket and an unfinished world (no stump matching the empty eye), then the stone keep — the tower he leaves — left behind on the headland. Those four photos are finished. We have **not** turned them into moving clips yet.

## Stairs sit

The stairs sit — him sitting on the stairs — needed a reshoot for face and body. A close-up of the feet cleared the old invented boots, then the sitting photo cleared. Soft note: five pin-like toes. Stairs motion is still ahead. Those frames are still photos only.

## Help landed as a cut

Help is no longer one crowded wide of robot, fox, and bird. Wide frames that tried to hold all three kept inventing a crow or dropping an animal, so we closed help as a **cut sequence**: separate shots edited together, not one wide picture. That sequence is a mid two-shot of the stuck petrel and fox, Rustboy alone looking down, plus closer cutaways of wire, hands, and the fox at the frame edge. Two short moving clips cleared from that path — the look-down and the petrel-fox mid. Purpose means help is now pictured. Those clips are finished locally. They are **not** folded into the screening reel yet.
