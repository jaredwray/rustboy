Rustboy is a small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket. Partway up a spiral stone stair inside the keep, he sits down on the steps. He leans a little forward, his forearms toward his knees. His gaze goes back down the stair, toward the dark well below. Pale light falls from high above, with dust in it.

This is a revisit. A post on 24 October was the first to show this still and clip. Back then, it sat in an older cut of the film. Our climb post last week glanced at this rest, and yesterday's post told a different, earlier sit. Today this later rest gets its own post, in the 5 November film.

The keep is a dead fortress on the coast. The team calls this tall stone shaft the well of light, and it holds no water. Just before this shot, he climbs toward that light. The climb carried a small hope that a job might wait at the top.

Here he stops partway, and nothing above calls to him. His question about his purpose stays open.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption for this shot reads: "Partway up, he sits on the spiral stair and rests, saying nothing."
