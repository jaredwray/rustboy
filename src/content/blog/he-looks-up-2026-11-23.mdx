At the foot of a rusted spiral stair, a small robot stands alone and tips his head back. His name is Rustboy, and his one green eye sits beside an empty socket. Around him rises a tall stone shaft deep inside the keep. He looks up a column of pale light that pours down from an opening at the top. The team calls this shaft the well of light, though it holds no water.

This is a revisit. The still and the clip first ran in October, when the shot joined an older, shorter cut. Yesterday's post ended by pointing at this look-up. Today we stay with it in the film as it plays now.

The keep offered him a dead phone, a light behind storm glass, a caged lever, and a wall of badges, including a child's visitor badge. None of these answers gave him a purpose. His upward look holds a hope that a purpose might wait above. For now he still does not know what he is for.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption here is: "At the foot of a spiral service stair he looks straight up a tall column of light."

In the published film, the look-up plays at about 1:00 to 1:06, after the visitor badge. Next, another shot in the same shaft shows him seated on a metal landing. The climb comes after that.

We made no new pictures for today's post. Both pictures already ran on the blog, and the shot already plays in the film.
