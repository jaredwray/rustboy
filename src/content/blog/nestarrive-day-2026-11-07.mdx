## How we made it

Rustboy is built from the approved character sheet (the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks), used as drawn and never flipped. As in the last two shots, his body comes from one three-quarter view and his head from another, turned toward the nest. That's the only acting. He doesn't crouch, lean, reach or lie down.

The place he's standing is a wet rock hollow with sparse grass around the rim; the petrel and flake sit in it, close. The nest in the next shot, where he lies down, is denser with twigs — same site and bird, different nest material. We kept that soft continuity rather than rebuilding the twigs today. The sky is a little brighter than on the shelf shot before it, which we also accepted. At this size the petrel reads as a small dark shape, harder to see in the hollow.

We locked the still first, then grew the clip with only the surroundings moving. Our quality check compared every frame against that still: Rustboy, the bird and the flake never change.
