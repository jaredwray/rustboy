Rising water fills a narrow cut in the wet rock. A four-legged fox is cut off by scrap and the tide. Above, a storm-petrel is caught in wire. Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, pauses at the edge of that water — then steps in, knowing it may cost him.

In the film as it stands, he works the wire with his hands. He holds the scrap clear so the fox can reach high ground, and then the bird lifts free. Then he hauls himself back out of the water. Helping other lives is where he begins to find a purpose.
