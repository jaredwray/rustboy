Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, has just stepped out through the doorway at the top of the keep, the dead coastal data fortress where he woke up. For the first time he is standing outside it, on bare bedrock that is wet with sea fog. One foot is out on the open stone. One hand is still resting on the weathered door stone beside him. His eye is looking ahead, into the gray, not back at where he came from. He doesn't move on yet.

Why that matters: this is the first time he has been outside the place that made him. Everything he knows is behind that door, and none of it turned out to be a reason to exist. So he keeps a hand on the only thing he knows while he looks at everything he doesn't. Around him the world keeps moving, with mist drifting past, spray in the air, and water dripping off the door frame. He stays still. The film hasn't decided for him yet.

## How we made it

The shot uses the approved character sheet (the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks) as drawn, never flipped: spherical rust head, one green eye on the viewer's left, a dark empty socket, nose, mouth slit, chest port, rounded feet. We matched the gray light, salt mist and wet sheen of the hut at the top of the stair, so the step out reads as the same cold morning, and set it up to hand off to the outdoor path that follows.

As with the last few shots, we locked the look of the still first, then grew the clip from it with only the surroundings moving. Our quality check compared every frame against that still, and not one of Rustboy's pixels changed in any of the clip's frames, including the hand on the door.

## What it sounds like

The doorway shot ends on an open, unfinished musical note. This shot doesn't answer it. The music simply holds what the doorway left hanging and lets it fade, with no new melody and no resolving note. Under it, outdoor air rises and a very low, distant surf comes in, handing over to the path. Because Rustboy doesn't move, there are no footsteps.

It's a quiet moment, softer than the doorway before it and the path after it.
