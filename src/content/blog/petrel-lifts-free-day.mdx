Below a nest ledge, squall water keeps climbing the marked rock in a narrow tide channel. Rustboy, the leftover scrap kid with one green eye and a sealed rust face, has dragged a scrap barrier out of the way. A four-legged fox scrambles through the gap and up to the high rock. He stays braced low in the rising water. A plate on his body snags on the wire and tears.

Then the storm-petrel he spent so long unwinding from that wire lifts off the ledge. It flies low over the surf. Up on the high rock, the fox looks back.

He has spent the whole film looking for a purpose. Here help is something he does, and it costs him. He frees both animals without owning either one, so the fox isn't his and the bird isn't his. It doesn't fix him, either. The torn plate is the price of helping, not a reward, and this isn't the end of the story.

## How the team made it

The team made a finished photo of the release and short moving clips of it and of the path opening. A finished photo is one locked frame, and a short clip is a few seconds of motion. In these clips only the water moves. Rustboy, the fox and the bird stay exactly as they are in the approved photos. When the team tried to animate whole pictures, the robot kept standing up and staring at the camera, so they moved only the water.

The bird took some work too. The first two tries came out looking like a crow, so instead of rolling the dice again, the team painted a small petrel into the best take. They also dropped a version where the bird drifted out of frame, because it looked like it was fading into the rock.

## The new cut, with sound

A progress cut is the single reel that lines up every finished shot in story order and carries the music mix. The new one runs about 169 seconds. Listen at the end. As the surge swells, the servo lets go and the little melody that runs through the film stops on its third note. After that, the last drops settle on metal, there's one soft servo tick, and then open wind. The chord never resolves before the final hold. There's no brass and no big sting.
