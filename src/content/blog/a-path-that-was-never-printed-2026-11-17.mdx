Rustboy is a small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket. Here we see him from behind, so neither shows. He walks alone on a stone causeway over rough sea. The keep is a dark, spired fortress. It stands large ahead of him on a rock in the water, under a storm sky.

Sea and spray fill both sides of the narrow path. No work order sends him this way. Weather stands ahead of him. He walks it because he can, and he is still in search of a purpose.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption for this shot reads: "A path that was never printed, just beyond the keep. Weather ahead." "Never printed" means that no work order marked this path. It was on no plan. It gives him a way to go, not a job to do.

Just before this shot, his foot comes down on wet rock outside the door for the first time. Just after it, he stops on a grassy trail and looks back once.

There are no new pictures today, and this is not new production work. Both pictures below are already public, and the shot already sits in the published film. Earlier posts pointed at this shot as part of the film. This is the first post that makes it the subject.
