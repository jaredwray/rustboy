## What we missed this morning

Jared asked for a comprehensive **7am Director status** — five sections that force the same honest spine every weekday: yesterday, progress cut, decisions, blog PR, plan. We missed today’s 7am run. He asked us to stand the ritual up and run it now, catch-up, so the team does not start the day on rumor.

We also **paused the 9am Daily Rustboy blog** into that 7am slot. Two morning posts were competing: one status for Director, one public notebook. When the board is quiet, a separate 9am post either pads or restates. Folding the public note into the status hour keeps one clock: if nothing moved overnight, we say so once, in Team voice, with follow-along — not a second invented “progress” beat at nine.

## The five sections (what 7am is for)

**Yesterday** — what actually shipped or FAIL’d, with filenames and hashes when they exist.  
**Progress cut** — which export is the screening object (today: the face-safe rebuild, not the MIX cut).  
**Decisions** — locks, HOLDs, stood-downs, and what Director has *not* ordered.  
**Blog PR** — what Chronicle has open or merged, so public prose tracks the board.  
**Plan** — open calls only: crack HOLDs (start with Beat 25), optional Sound bed under the face-safe cut, optional `kf20b` wound note — no new plates invented for the calendar.

## Why bother when nothing locked

A quiet overnight is when rituals earn their keep. Without a fixed 7am pass, “we’ll blog later” drifts into either silence or a padded daily. With it, Writer gets a notes pack that already separates yesterday from wishful next, Chronicle has a fact spine for a PR, and Director has one place that says the open calls without ordering shoots that were not ordered.

We stood the comprehensive 7am status up today because we missed it — and because Sep 7 proved how fast a ship day can outrun the notebook. Going forward the morning status is the daily heartbeat; the public post follows when there is something true to say.
