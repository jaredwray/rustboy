Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, is out of the water now. He sits low and still on a wet rock shelf on the coast, after hauling himself out of the tide channel. With two fingertips he touches the rim of his empty eye socket. His hand stays there; the socket stays open. The four-legged fox is out of danger now, a small shape on rock a long way off, and she keeps her distance.

He has just helped her, and he has just helped a storm-petrel caught in scrap wire. Helping other lives is where his purpose begins, but help does not complete him. This is a quiet moment with what is still missing: the empty socket, and the unfinished world. Neither fills the other.

We covered this moment in October, and nothing here is new work. This post looks at it again in its place in the full published film, right after the rescue.

Next comes the walk away: he's on a wet rocky path beside the sea, and the keep, the dead coastal fortress where he woke up, sits small on a headland behind him. He's leaving it, having helped, and he doesn't go back. Up ahead and higher on the rocks, the fox keeps to a path of her own rather than following him.
