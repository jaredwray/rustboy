Rustboy is a small rusted robot. He stands alone in a peeling corridor deep inside the keep, beside a wall of curling staff ID badges. His round head faces us, with one green eye and one empty socket. A round port sits in his chest. He studies the human faces on the badges, and none of them are his.

Earlier posts touched this wall with older pictures, and an October post told the badge moment that comes right after this shot. Today we come back to the wall for the wider shot that is in the film now.

This is the fourth wrong answer the keep offers him, after the dead phone, the light behind the storm glass, and the caged lever. He finds no badge of his own among them. His purpose remains unfound.

The team keeps a storyboard, a board of planned shots in story order. It gives this shot a short caption: “A wall of curling staff ID badges. None of the faces are his. Wrong answer four.”

In the published film, the wall plays at about 0:48 to 0:54. It comes just after he leans in at the caged lever. Next, he stops at one child's visitor badge on the same wall.

There are no new pictures today. This is not new production work. Both pictures below are already public, and the shot is already in the published film.
