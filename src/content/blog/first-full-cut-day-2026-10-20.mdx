Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye, lies on his back on wet rock below a petrel's nest, with the sea behind him. This is before he meets the fox. Much later, after the rescue, he has climbed out of the tide channel and sits low on a flat rock shelf, small and still. He raises one thin hand and touches the rim of his empty eye socket, without filling it or covering it. The help happened, and what he's missing is still missing. Then he walks along a wet coast path away from the keep, the dead fortress where he woke up, with his torn plate still on him. He doesn't look back.

Those three moments were the last places in the film where his look switched partway through. Now they match too, so the whole film shows one Rustboy, the one from his approved character sheet, from the first frame to the last.

## What the team did

A **character sheet** is the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks. Over the past few days the team has been rebuilding every shot where he'd drifted from it. These three were the last. Each one has a finished photo, a single locked frame that sets how the scene looks, and a short clip, a few seconds of motion grown from that photo.

To be plain about the motion: in all three clips, Rustboy, the bird and the sky stay still. Only the sea moves.
