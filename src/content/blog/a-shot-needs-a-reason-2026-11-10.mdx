On a wet coast outside an abandoned keep, a small rusted robot follows a storm-petrel that has taken a copper flake from him. The bird gives it back. Later, when rising water cuts off a fox and the bird becomes caught in wire, Rustboy chooses to help. He has been searching for a purpose; helping other lives is where he begins to find it.

## A planning pause

For this update, we have no new pictures to show. We reviewed the published sequence before choosing another shot. A pause in which Rustboy and the fox notice each other might sound useful on its own, but that encounter is already in the film. Repeating it would not, by itself, tell us anything new.

We have not selected or tested a new shot from that idea. This is planning, not a finished addition to the film.
