Night has settled on a wet stretch of rocky coast. Rustboy — a small rusted robot, one eye a steady green, the other an empty socket — is by himself on that dark stone. Behind him the sea is calm; above him the sky is clear and full of stars. No one else is in the frame. He tips his head until the green eye meets one star that stands out a little from the others. The star does not answer.

Inside the keep, the dead coastal fortress where he woke, he once tried a similar lining-up: his eye almost matched a stubborn light on a server, as if that glow might tell him what he was for. It never did. Since then he has begun to find purpose by helping other lives. Help did not make him whole, and he does not need a light in the sky to give him a job. Tonight the star stays silent, and he no longer needs it to reply.

Our 21 October post already introduced this shot, against an older cut of the film at the time. Today we look at it again in the published version. No fresh pictures are included here, and the published reel is still the one from 5 November.

## The night still and clip

What you see first is a single finished photograph of the scene, then a few silent seconds of motion built from it. Both have been on the site since October. In the still he stands toward the right of the frame, facing a little to the left, on wet dark rock. Both the green eye and the empty socket can be seen. The night light cools his rust, though he still reads as rust-brown, and the horizon sits low over a calm sea.
