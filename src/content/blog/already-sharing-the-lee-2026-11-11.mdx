Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, has followed a storm-petrel to wet rock under its nest. The bird gives back the copper flake it took from him. Soon after, he and a four-legged fox notice each other across that rock. A squall drives robot, fox, and petrel into the same lee out of the wind. Later, rising water squeezes the fox into a narrow channel while the bird fights scrap wire above — and Rustboy chooses to help. Helping other lives is where he begins to find a purpose.

## What the published film already covers

We looked again at the stretch that follows the nest return and the fox encounter, asking whether a new shot belonged there. In the film as it stands, the next plates are already present: the three of them sharing shelter from the squall, then the rising-tide channel that leads toward help. Remaking the lee, inventing another pause after the fox, or treating the channel as unpublished would invent a gap the public cut does not have.

So there was no verified missing action to shoot for this update, and no new plate selected. A later day might still find a real hole elsewhere in the sequence. That would need a verified native source — at least 1280 by 720, without upscaling — with pack-true identity throughout. Pixel dimensions on an existing file are not proof of that provenance, and generating a background alone would not invent a story action the film does not lack. Until such a source is in hand, we hold rather than invent or enlarge.
