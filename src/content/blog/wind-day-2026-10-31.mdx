Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, has reached the top of the spiral service stair inside the keep, the dead coastal data fortress where he woke up. The stair ends in a square metal-framed hatch in the floor of a small stone hut, and he is standing on that floor now, just past the lip of the well. Beside him is an open doorway full of pale gray daylight. Wind and salt mist come in through it, and a few flakes of rust ride the draft over his head and drop away down the well behind him. He doesn't look back.

Why that matters: this is where he finds out there's nobody at the top. No maker, no other pair of green eyes, no job waiting, just weather. He's been climbing toward the light since he chose to stand on the landing, and this is what the light turns out to be. He stops and takes that in. The next shot, which was already in the film, shows him from behind stepping out through that doorway anyway.

## Two new shots

There are two new shots in today's cut, one after the other.

The first is the last flight: Rustboy climbing the final stretch of stair toward the square hatch, worn down and slower than before. It was finished earlier this week but didn't make it into the full film in time, so yesterday's post was a quiet one with nothing new to show. Now it's in.

The second is new today: the stop in the hut. Without it the film went from Rustboy climbing in the dark straight to Rustboy already leaving, and nobody ever saw his face at the top. Our story bible (the team's written outline of the film) has this moment: at the threshold he stops, wind, salt, the whole gray world, and then he steps through. This shot is the stop.

In the new cut, the last flight runs from about 1:43 to 1:49 and the hut stop from about 1:49 to 1:55, right before the threshold. Nothing else in the film changed; everything after them just starts later.

## How we made them

Both shots use the approved character sheet (the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks) as drawn, never flipped: spherical rust head, one green eye on the viewer's left, a dark empty socket, nose, mouth slit, chest port, rounded feet. His eye stays as dim and steady as it was on the landing, so the whole climb reads as one tired stretch.

For each shot we locked the look of the still first, then grew the clip from it with only the environment moving. Our quality check compared every frame against the character sheet, and Rustboy's pixels didn't change in a single frame of either clip.

## What it sounds like

The new stop in the hut sits quiet, at about −25.8 LUFS (a loudness measure) against about −21.4 LUFS for the whole cut. The loudest peak anywhere in the cut is −7.1 dBTP, well clear of clipping.
