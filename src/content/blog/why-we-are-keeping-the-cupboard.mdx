*RUSTBOY making-of — first public post*  
*Team Rust Boy AI · board truth as of 7 September 2026*

We're remaking the early-2000s Rustboy short as a silent dark fairy tale for festival and Emmy-bar scrutiny. The cupboard is where that work lives in public: locked plates, named kills, and HOLD freezes that are still waiting. It is a record. It is not merch, not a trailer of frames we wish were finished.

## Keeping the fails

The short only works if he is the same leftover kid from the table to the horizon. One spherical rust head. Viewer-left: a warm phosphor-green optic. Viewer-right: an empty socket. No mouth. Crooked copper plate. Thin ball-joint limbs. The KF02 table still is the body lock we measure everything else against.

A lot of takes never make that face. They grin. They flip which eye is live. They morph mid-shot, turn toward the lens, or paint the optic amber. On the coast the same bar shows up as an empty-beak petrel or a fox with four legs — or with the stump on the wrong hind. Those are kills. Smaller stuff (a 3+thumb digit count) stays a note, and notes stay notes.

When a take dies, the next try gets a pickup name (`kfXXb`, `kfXXc`). The fail keeps the filename that earned the FAIL. We do not overwrite a dead plate to make the shelf look clean. The cupboard is how we keep the bar visible: the cut is only as honest as the kills that taught it.

### HOLD freeze — where motion stopped

Beat 18 grass and beats 21–25 (fox through lee, absence, keep-behind, horizon) are HOLD freeze: stills may PASS-as-still; motion is not locked. Shooter and QC stood down on those beats. The stump / facing / amber / four-leg family stays on the kill sheet, not in the cut. When we crack the HOLDs, fox stump side-flips and mush on current freeze stills are named unresolved — they do not become motion by sitting on the shelf.

## Continuity on the record

Two laws stay written next to the locks:

The plate wound after the petrel theft is metal and copper tear only. No blood.

The fox stump is left hind only. Four-leg foxes and opposite-leg flips are kills.

Those rules are why some stills never graduate. Keeping the bad ones visible is how we stop arguing with memory.

## What this post is not

Not a plot dump of Acts II–III past the locked teaser bible, the adventure bible, and the board. Not orders to Shooter, QC, or Sound. Not a claim that HOLD freezes are locked motion. Not a silent live publish — media for this post lives at `media.rustboy.ai` on the `rustboy-media` bucket (eleven first-post keys); the Astro site at [jaredwray/rustboy](https://github.com/jaredwray/rustboy) ships the page when a PR merges. Stream stays held. Full-archive bulk upload waits.

The cupboard stays open because the film is the locks *and* the kills. One face. Empty socket dark. Grin still illegal.

Follow along on [rustboy.ai](https://rustboy.ai) as we keep writing the board down — locks, kills, and the HOLDs that are still waiting.
