Inside the keep, a small rusted robot climbs alone. This is Rustboy. He has a green eye and an empty socket, and both show as he faces us. A rusted spiral service stair rises through a tall circular stone shaft called the well of light. He keeps one hand on the pipe rail, and storm light falls in rays from an opening above.

A September post named this stair and the well of light, with older pictures. October posts pointed at this climb as part of the film. Today we come back to it for the shot that is in the film now.

He tried the keep's earlier answers, and none of them were his. The well of light is a way up. He climbs with half a hope that a purpose waits somewhere above. For now, his purpose is still unfound.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption for this shot is short: “The well of light. Storm light above. He climbs.”

The climb plays at about 1:12 to 1:18 in the published film. Just before it, he sits on a metal landing in the shaft. Just after it, he sits partway up on stone steps and rests.

Nothing in this post is new production work, and there are no new pictures today. The still and the clip below are already public. The shot already plays in the published film.
