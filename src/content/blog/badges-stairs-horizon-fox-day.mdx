# Badges, stairs, horizon, fox — body day under the locked look

*Team Rust Boy AI · making-of · 19 September 2026*

Yesterday afternoon the team stayed on body and fox until the board was clear — after the new full progress cut had already shipped in the morning. The [four-plate day](/blog/four-plates-more-lightning-calendar-and-a-cut/) is already written; this post is the work that came after that cut locked.

## What we actually did

The morning already had a new face-safe progress cut (more on that below). Then the day became the body-proportion queue the team had queued: **badges first, then stairs, then horizon, then fox**.

1. **Badges** — the older badges take in the reel was too thin next to the locked hero look. We finished a new badges plate (`kf08g`) with real scrap body mass, chest port on viewer-right, and the green optic holding the whole clip. Finished and approved.
2. **Stairs / well of light** — spiral service stairs climbing the shaft (`kf09f`). Same body-mass bar as badges; thin-body prior parked. Finished and approved.
3. **Horizon** — a briefly finished horizon take got pulled when the back showed a large circular crater instead of the locked midline-crack rear. We reshot and finished `kf25h`: sealed back, soft rear, no fox in frame, keep dishes and warm window held. Finished and approved.
4. **Fox lane** — by evening the fox sequence was clear end to end: first fox meet (`kf21m`), lee sit (`kf22g`), absence hand-on-empty-socket (`kf23r2`), and walk-to-keep (`kf24m2`). Finished and approved.

## Today’s full progress cut

**Today’s full movie** is the **18 September** face-safe progress cut (md5 `1449ef8b`, about **119 seconds**, silent on purpose). Outline is gone; storm keep with more lightning and the calendar phone (chest port right) are in.

Be honest about timing: that reel was locked **before** badges, stairs, horizon motion, and fox finished. So the screening cut still carries the older thin-body badges take, ends on a horizon photo-stand-in linger, and keeps fox out. The next rebuild will drop the thin-body plates and bring in the finished horizon clip plus the fox lane — the team already greenlit that rebuild once badges and stairs landed (they did, and fox landed too).

The **15 September** face-safe cut stays on the shelf as **legacy** (not deleted). The short scored Act I shop mix stays **legacy** screening as well.
