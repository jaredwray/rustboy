Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, is alone halfway up a service stair inside the keep, the dead coastal data fortress where he woke up. He's climbing toward the top when his power sags and he has to sit down on a wet stone landing. A crack in the wall beside him weeps a thin silver line of rain, and a shallow puddle lies on the floor by his feet. He looks at it. The lamp-light from far below shifts in the water into soft shapes of places he's been: the empty table he woke up on, the cracked phone that gave him his first "job", the caged lever he chose not to pull. It isn't a dream or a flashback. It's just light in water.

Why that matters: he woke up with no purpose and has spent the first part of the film trying the keep's answers, and every one came up empty. Here they all show up at once, in a puddle, and none of them answers him. It's the feeling of being leftover on purpose. Then, in shots that were already in the film, he gets back up because he can and climbs on to the door of light at the top.

## How we made it

The background is the same wet landing and well of light as the shot where he sits down, seen from higher up and looking down, with a stone ledge added for him to sit on and the puddle in front of him.

Rustboy took three tries. On the first, he seemed to be looking at the camera instead of into the puddle, the shapes in the water looked like floating objects rather than reflected light, and the edge of the frame cut him off. On the second, his head tipped down toward the puddle, which read well, but our quality check (QC, the frame-by-frame check against his character sheet, the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks) failed it: his head was cocked on his neck by about 8°, against a limit of 1°, so it read as a tilted head rather than a look down. The third try, the one in the film, sits his head level on his body and passed. We also redid the memory shapes as soft, pale-gold reflected lamp-light.

He's built from the character sheet as drawn, never flipped, sitting on the ledge with both feet visible, and he's much bigger in the frame than in the flooded sub-level shot: about 482 pixels tall against 299. His own body never shows in the puddle, because a body reflection would mean mirroring his image, and we never mirror him. Only the lamp-light is in the water. The final take passed QC on character, his one eye, colour and that no-reflection rule, and we checked by eye that his legs weren't flipped.

The clip was grown from the finished still, and the new shot went into the cut from the full-quality master copy of the clip rather than a compressed one.

## What it sounds like

The rain leak carries over from the shot before, along with the hollow air of the stairwell, a small trickle running down the wall, and a soft patter that follows the shimmer on the puddle. The puddle shimmers steadily rather than rippling from single drops, so there's no single drip sound, since there's nothing on screen to time one to. There's no music on purpose: the door-of-light music arrives right after, and a hint of it here would crowd it. He's sitting still, so there are no footsteps or body sounds. It's the quietest moment in the cut, about -25.7 LUFS (a loudness measure), against about -20.7 LUFS for the whole cut.

## What's still soft

- With his head level, he reads more as looking across at the puddle than down into it. The tipped-down version is the one that failed for the cocked neck.
- The pose comes from an eye-level drawing on the character sheet, but the camera looks down on him, so the angles don't quite match.
- He's perched on the ledge rather than fully seated, and his thighs look short and flat.
- The table shape in the puddle is cut off at the puddle's edge.
- The soft patter fades out a little slower than the shimmer in the picture.
