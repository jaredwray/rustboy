In the last picture of the film, a small rusted robot named Rustboy walks toward open weather in the morning. Then the film holds on black for about one second. Next comes the credits crawl, the end titles that scroll up the screen. Slanted rust-orange credits rise into a starfield. They settle on a small rust-orange rustboy.ai, centered on black with faint stars.

This is a revisit. An October post already showed the last frame of these credits, and an earlier October post told a simpler end card. The new part today is the clip of the crawl. It appears on the blog for the first time.

Over the film, Rustboy learns that his purpose is to help other lives. The last picture leaves him after that help, still not whole. The film does not show where he goes next. It cuts to black, rolls the team credits, and closes on rustboy.ai.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption for this last cell reads: "A short black hold, then the end card and the team credits crawl."

In the published film, which runs 5:20, the black hold sits at about 4:43 to 4:44. The crawl runs from about 4:44 to the end at 5:20. The walk toward open weather comes just before it. Nothing follows the crawl.

There are no new pictures today, and this is not new production work. The still and the clip are already public. The ending already plays in the published film.
