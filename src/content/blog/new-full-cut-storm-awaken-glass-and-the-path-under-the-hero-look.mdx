## What we did that day

The hero look was already law. On 15 September we put it into the picture for real.

We finished a storm plate with no character in frame (`kf01-storm-b`), then an awaken clip under the hero pack (`kf02-awaken-j`) after killing earlier takes that grew a second eye, an ear lamp, or the wrong amber glow. Then we rebuilt the full-board screening reel. QC scored it. Director approved it. That cut is what you should watch today.
