A small rusted robot sits by himself on a wet stone step and looks straight at us. He is Rustboy, with one green eye, one empty socket, and a round port in his chest. His hands rest on the step at his sides. His rounded feet rest on the step below. The stone glistens, and a little mist hangs in the air.

This is a revisit. This still and clip first appeared on 28 September. They ran in the post "A fox across open air, and the stairs that cost him," when the film was an older cut. Yesterday's post covered the rest that comes just before this shot. Today we give this shot a post of its own, set in the 5 November film.

We are deep in the keep, the dead fortress where the film takes place, on a dark service stair. Earlier, he climbed toward light in the hope of a job at the top. Nothing up there called him. In this shot he stays down on the stone, close to us, with his eye lit. He is still only partway up, and the keep still gives him no purpose.

The team's plan for this shot speaks of his power dipping. On screen, that dip reads only as stillness. He sits, and he holds still.

In the published film, this shot runs from about 1:24 to 1:30. Just before it, he rests on the spiral stair. Just after it, he looks down into a rain puddle. Two days ago we wrote about an earlier sit, up on a metal landing, looking up. Yesterday's rest came after the climb, with him bent forward and looking down. Here he sits upright on wet stone and faces us.

We made nothing new for this post. The still and clip went public in September, and the shot sits in the published film.
