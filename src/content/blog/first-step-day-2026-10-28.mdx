Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, is alone deep inside the keep, the dead coastal data fortress where he woke up. He is on the first tread of a tall spiral service stair — a stone-and-steel shaft of pale god rays and drifting rust motes. The film calls this place the well of light; it is not a water well. After looking straight up the column of light, he puts his left foot on the first tread and begins the climb. He is closer in the frame than the look-up — the shaft still tall around him, but the first step is real now. Only the room moves: the god rays shimmer a little, and dust motes drift.

Why that matters: he woke up with no purpose and has been trying every answer the keep offers. The look-up was the hope that purpose might still be waiting above. The first step is the choice to climb anyway — toward the door of light — before the later mid-stair sag teaches him that no assignment is coming to meet him. He takes the stairs.

## How we made it

The finished still is him with the left foot on the first tread (a small tip-back lean), green optic on the viewer-left side of his head, whole uncut limbs, solid rather than skinny, closer and lower than the look-up so the foot plant reads. He's built from the approved character sheet (the master set of pictures that defines exactly how he looks) as drawn, never flipped or stretched: one green eye, dark empty socket, nose, mouth slit, chest port, rounded feet.

The still passed QC (our frame-by-frame check against that sheet) on size, head angle, eye and socket, arms, hips, feet, face details, and the first-step read. The clip passed too: its first frame matches the finished still exactly, and Rustboy — including his optic — doesn't change in a single frame; only rays, motes and a little paper or haze move. We locked the look and promoted it before it went into motion. The clip uses environment-only motion: the room breathes while he stays still on purpose.

## What it sounds like

The new progress cut with this shot in story order lands at about −21.1 LUFS (a loudness measure) for the whole reel, about 4:20 long. Hold the searching quiet — no theme celebrating a purpose found. He's frozen on the first tread, so there are no footstep or body sounds here; the climb already in the film still awaits.
