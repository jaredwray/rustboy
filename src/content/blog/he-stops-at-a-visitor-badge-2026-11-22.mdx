Deep inside the keep, a small rusted robot stops in a peeling corridor. This is Rustboy, with his green eye and his empty socket. A wall of curling ID badges fills the space beside him, and he looks toward the human faces. A child's VISITOR badge hangs on the left. No face there is his.

This is a revisit. An October post already showed this still and this clip, when the shot first went into an older, shorter cut. A post earlier this week told the wider badge wall just before it. Today we come back to the closer stop as it plays in the film now.

The dead phone, the light behind storm glass, and the caged lever came first. The wide wall was the fourth wrong answer the keep gave him, and this closer stop makes it personal. Someone small was welcomed here once, but the badge is not his. He still hasn't found what he is for.

The storyboard is the team's board of planned shots in story order. Its caption for this shot reads: "He stops at a child's VISITOR badge, his green eye caught in the dusty plastic. Neither that face nor any other is him."

In the published film, the stop plays at about 0:54 to 1:00, just after the wider badge wall. Next, Rustboy looks straight up a tall column of light from the foot of a spiral stair.

There are no new pictures today, and this post adds no new production work. The still and the clip below are already public, and the stop already plays in the film.
