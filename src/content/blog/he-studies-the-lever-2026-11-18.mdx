Deep inside the keep, a dead coastal fortress full of old servers, a small rusted robot stands alone at a rusty control console. This is Rustboy. He has a round head, one green eye, and one empty socket, and his face is toward us. Right next to him, a large red safety lever stands in a wire cage. He studies the handle but does not pull it.

An October post already told this lever moment. Today we come back to it for the closer shot that is in the film now.

The lever is the third wrong answer the keep offers him, after the dead phone and the light behind storm glass. It looks like an important task. But the work was meant for larger hands, and those hands are gone. He gives the lever his full attention and still leaves it unpulled. His purpose is still unfound.

On the team's storyboard, the board of planned shots in story order, this shot carries the caption: "A red lever under a wire cage. He studies it and leaves it alone. Wrong answer three."

The shot plays at about 0:36 to 0:42 in the published film. Just before it, his palm meets the storm glass, and his eye almost matches a server light. A wider view of him beside the cage follows.

There are no new pictures today. Nothing in this post is new production work. The still and the clip are already public, and the shot is already in the published film.
