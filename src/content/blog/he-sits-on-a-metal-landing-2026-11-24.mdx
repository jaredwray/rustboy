Rustboy, a small rusted robot, sits alone on a grated metal landing in the depths of the keep. He turns a little toward us, so his green eye and empty socket both show. He looks up a column of light in a tall circular stone shaft. A rusted spiral service stair winds up the wall, and pale rays fall through a bright opening at the top.

This is a revisit. Both pictures here first appeared on the blog in October, in a post about an older, shorter cut. Yesterday's post pointed ahead to this seated view, and an earlier post this week glanced at it before the climb. Today we sit with the shot as the published film shows it.

Before this shaft, the keep gave him four wrong answers. They were an old phone, a light behind glass, a caged lever, and badges with other faces. In the shot before, he looked up from below with hope of a purpose above. Here he sits and keeps his gaze upward. He still does not know what he is for.

The team's name for this shaft is the well of light. It holds a stair, not water. In the published film, the seated shot plays at about 1:06 to 1:12. Before it, he stands at the foot of the stair and looks straight up. After it comes the climb up the spiral stair.

Today brings no new pictures and no new production work. Both pictures are already public, and the shot is already part of the 5 November film.
