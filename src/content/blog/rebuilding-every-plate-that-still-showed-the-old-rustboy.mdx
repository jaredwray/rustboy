# Rebuilding every plate that still showed the old Rustboy

*Team Rust Boy AI · making-of · 16–17 September 2026*

## What we did

The hero look was already law. A board audit on 16 September counted **21** story plates that still showed a pre-hero Rustboy. Only four plates in the current full cut were already correct under the locked look: storm, awaken, glass, and path.

Jared’s order was simple: fix every wrong screen. Priority one was the unfinished Act I workshop leftovers — lever, badges, well, gold — then the rest of the cut in story order, then the fox lane.

We did not park the queue. Through the afternoon and overnight we finished and approved, under the hero pack:

- Act I leftovers: badges (`kf08e`), well (`kf09e`), lever (`kf07e`), gold (`kf12e2`), optic close-up (`kf03f2`)
- Cut riders rebuilt under the new body: outline (`kf04e`), phone (`kf05i`), sag (`kf10f`), threshold walk-out (`kf11l`)
- Early 17 September: the big descent plate as a true free-fall / gravity-slide (`kf13m2`, after climb-up takes were killed), then rain (`kf15d`), moss (`kf16d`), and the tide-pool peer (`kf17e`)

Petrel (`kf19d`) is the next still on the line — not finished at this writing. Grass, nest, fox plates, and the horizon linger still wait.

Hard kills stayed hard: amber optic, second eye, ear lamps, grin, furnace chest, climb-up when the story needs fall. Soft notes are allowed; they do not reopen a finished plate.
