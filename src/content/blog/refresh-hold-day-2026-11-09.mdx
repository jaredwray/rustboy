Rustboy, the small rusted robot with one green eye and one empty socket, is lying on his back on wet rock under a storm-petrel's nest. Earlier in the film the bird took a copper flake from him, and he followed it. Now the bird gives it back: the flake lies on the rock beside him, and the petrel stays close.

In the story this is care without owning. The bird returns what it took. It is almost a job for Rustboy, but not yet a purpose.

## What we tried

The return shot is already in the film. On 7 November we worked on a refresh of it, because of a newer shot we've made locally to go just before it. That newer shot isn't in the public cut. It shows Rustboy already lying on his back beside the nest, with the flake still in the bird's bill. Placed next to it, the existing return doesn't match: the bird is much larger, the nest is a thick tangle of twigs instead of a shallow rock hollow, and the sea is stormier.

So we tried to rebuild the return in the newer shot's place and light, changing only one thing: the flake moved out of the bird's bill and onto the rock.

In our first version the flake came out far too big for something that came from his chest. The second fixed the size, and then the problem showed: in that wide view, a flake at its true size is tiny on screen, about 14 pixels long at normal viewing size. It reads as a bright fleck, not clearly the piece the bird gave back. We tried it in six spots on the rock, and then tried moving in closer on the same image. Moving in made the picture soft and cut off parts of Rustboy we check on every shot. Our quality check held each attempt, so none of them went into the film.

None of the tested placements or the crop made the return clear enough. The next step is a new, closer camera view of the same place, with the same Rustboy taken from his character pack, so the flake can be seen at its real size.
